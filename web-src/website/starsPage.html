Some of my favourite astronomers/astrophysicists, and their contributions to astronomy:

Throughout history, a bunch of people have made tremendous contributions, even despite obstacles like The Church, who were harshly against any "blasphemous" ideas or attempts to challenge the religious consensus of the time.

An extensive article on the conflict between Galileo and the Catholic Church is a great example: Read the Galileo article

Galileo Galilei

Galileo used the telescope to observe mountains on the Moon, the phases of Venus, and the moons orbiting Jupiter.

Johannes Kepler

Kepler used Tycho Brahe's observations to establish that planets travel in elliptical orbits rather than perfect circles. Best known for his three laws of planetary motion.

Nicolaus Copernicus

Copernicus proposed the heliocentric model of the solar system, placing sun at the center of the universe, as oppose to the Earth. This was one of the major challenges against the church's geocentric belief, which was deemed infalliable.

Edwin Hubble

Hubble showed that Andromeda is a separate galaxy and provided evidence that the universe is expanding.
