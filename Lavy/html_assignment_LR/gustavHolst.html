Gustav Holst was an English composer, arranger and teacher whose curiosity reached far beyond the concert hall.

Inspired by astrology, Sanskrit texts and English folk song, he shaped music that feels both ancient and otherworldly. His best-known work, The Planets, gives each celestial body its own character—from Mars's relentless force to Neptune's vanishing horizon.

That suite made Holst one of astronomy's most memorable musical interpreters, influencing generations of film composers and science-fiction soundscapes.
